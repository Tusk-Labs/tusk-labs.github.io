WIDGET NEST

PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 23, 2026

Widget Nest ("the app") is made by Tusk Labs ("we"). It puts a decorative pixel-art pet on your home screen. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

THE SHORT VERSION

  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not run any server. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The app saves the following on your phone or tablet only:

  • the pet, terrain and widget size you chose;
  • the widget's animation state and the size your home screen gives the widget;
  • which items you have unlocked.

This information never leaves your device. It is excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

PURCHASES

Pets, terrains and packs are sold through Google Play's billing system. Google Play collects and processes your payment details; we never see your name, email address or card details. When you buy something, or when the app checks what you own, Google Play tells the app only which items your Google account owns and the status of each purchase. The app keeps that list on your device to unlock those items.

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. To request a refund, use Google Play.

INTERNET ACCESS

The app uses the internet only through the Google Play Billing Library, to show prices and to complete and confirm purchases. That library is provided by Google and may send technical information to Google to make purchases work; this is covered by Google's privacy policy.

SHARING

We do not sell, share or transfer any information about you, because we do not collect any.

CHILDREN

Widget Nest is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, children included: there is no account, no chat, no ads and no way to send us information from inside the app.

Items are bought through Google Play, which requires the Google account holder to complete each purchase. Parents can require their approval for purchases with Google Play's purchase authentication settings or with Google Family Link.

DELETING YOUR INFORMATION

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your device settings, to delete everything the app stored. Your purchases stay linked to your Google account, so they unlock again if you reinstall the app with the same account.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
